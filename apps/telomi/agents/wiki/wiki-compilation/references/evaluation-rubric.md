# Wiki compilation review

Compare anonymous Wiki outputs against the frozen Goal, Cornell Notes, previous Wiki and confirmed Topic Plan. Inspect the recorded stage inputs, outputs and native sessions when an omission or unsupported change needs explanation.

- Preserve source terminology, metric direction, experimental conditions and evidence identity. Do not trade factual fidelity for smoother prose.
- Objects retain specific mechanisms, procedures, reproduction conditions and limits. Concepts explain reusable ideas without merely duplicating objects.
- Every Cue receives an explicit object-layer disposition. Concept extraction must not create a separate route for abandoned Cue material.
- Topic navigation can link object or concept sections directly. Evaluate whether distinct relevant details remain discoverable; link counts alone are not coverage. A relationship does not automatically make neighboring sections direct Topic matches.
- Relations preserve grounded direction and meaning. Backlinks are navigation, not additional inferred semantic edges.
- A Topic-only reindex changes the navigation index and overview, preserving the underlying object, concept, evidence and relationship files.
- Examine failed, retried and cancelled stages. Partial or missing outputs cannot establish semantic success.

Prefer the output that preserves factual meaning and provides useful, nonredundant access to the requested knowledge. Record concrete missing or unsupported claims with page/section references. Evaluate cost and duration separately from semantic quality.
