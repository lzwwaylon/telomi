# Wiki Shard Builder Frozen Evaluation Rubric

Compare A and B against the frozen Goal, Topic Plan, Cornell Notes, Sources, and Evidence anchors.

Check that every Cornell Entry is covered by the aggregate citation union or explicitly deferred, factual prose remains supported by the supplied Evidence, Concept and Entity identities are coherent, Topic refs stay within scope, and no unsupported knowledge is introduced. The same Note may support distinct meaningful Entity and Concept pages. Reuse is not duplication.

Reject silent evidence loss, marker-only fake coverage, overlapping or artificial Concepts, invented relationships, invalid citations, missing source provenance, broken links, malformed frontmatter, or structurally invalid knowledge trees. Do not reward Page count or require a minimum Concept count. Only after semantic quality is tied, prefer lower failure rate, Token, Cost, Tool Calls, or Duration.
