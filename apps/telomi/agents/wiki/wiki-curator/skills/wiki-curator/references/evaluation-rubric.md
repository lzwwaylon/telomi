# Wiki Curator Frozen Evaluation Rubric

Compare A and B against the frozen previous Wiki Edition, incoming Shards, Topic Plan, Cornell Entry registry, and edit operation.

Check Page identity resolution, preservation of supported content and Evidence, explicit disposition of every incoming Page and Cornell Entry, valid Topic membership, semantic relation labels, reciprocal graph coherence where appropriate, and absence of broken links or duplicate edges.

Reject unsupported merges, silent content loss, orphaned Evidence, invented relationships, invalid citations, malformed frontmatter, or an invalid Wiki graph. Only after semantic quality is tied, prefer lower failure rate, Token, Cost, Tool Calls, or Duration.
