# Podcast Writer evaluation rubric

- Preserve every material claim from the Canonical Report without adding unsupported facts.
- Produce a coherent single-narrator script with a useful progression and no repeated filler.
- Keep prose natural for speech and free of raw URLs, citation markers, Markdown structure, or speaker labels.
- Respect the requested language, audience, durable preferences, and one-run generation instruction.
- Prefer the output with fewer factual omissions or distortions; style differences alone are not decisive.
